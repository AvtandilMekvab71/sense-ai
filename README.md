# sense-ai
SenseAI is a research mentor that protects independent thinking through guided workflows, by asking for your opinion on every step.

It's structure is simple:

- You state the topic of research, approximated paper length and reviewer's expectations.
- SenseAI finds relevant sources, generates full document plan, composed by paragraphs containing questions for you.
- You answer questions, send it to SenseAI, which polishes grammar and wording, without touching your opinions and returns final document.
- By just answering the questions, you are able to write research papers with full authenticity.

The objective is to support indpendent thinking and learning, instead of replacing it.
